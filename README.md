## English

Tarkam Arena is a 3D fighting game from the world of *tarkam* — the village tournaments of
Indonesia — where street workers, spirits, wildlife, satire and legends of the archipelago meet.

- **51 fighters**: professions (the red-light drifter, the minimarket cashier, the market thug…),
  mystic creatures (Kuntilanak, Pocong, Buto Ijo, Leak, Kuyang, Ogoh-ogoh, Reog…), Nusantara wildlife
  (orangutan, komodo, Sumatran tiger, bird of paradise…), city & satire (the corrupt official, the
  buzzer, the student protester, the ride-hailing driver, the sound-horeg dancer…), five secret
  legends (Nyi Roro Kidul, Gatotkaca, Si Pitung, Sangkuriang, Jaka Tarub) and three DLC packs.
- **Real fighting-game systems**: frame data, target combos, launchers and air combos, grabs and
  throw techs, counter hits, parries, dodges, motion-input specials, Rage and Adrenaline modes,
  team combos.
- **Signature moves**: three per fighter on the Jurus meter — summons, crowd charges, giant weapons,
  weather strikes, lift & slam, tornadoes, barrages, quakes and waves, each with its own cut-in and VFX.
- **Finishers**: every fighter has 3 Fatalities, 2 Brutalities, 2 Friendships, 1 Mercy and 1
  Legendary Finisher that needs a roaring crowd.
- **Modes**: 1 vs 1, 2 vs 2, Tag Battle, Free For All, Survival, a nine-chapter Story, the Tarkam Cup
  tournament, Online Ranked with rollback netcode, Clan Wars, Custom matches and Training.
- **18 living arenas** — from Pasar Becek and the Parliament steps to Borobudur, Mount Bromo, the
  sound-horeg carnival and the eve of Nyepi — with locals who trade, cheer, film, flee finishers and
  recognise fighters, plus arena events that hit the fighters.
- **Sound of the archipelago**: gamelan, angklung, degung, talempong, sasando, baleganjur, reog,
  dangdut koplo, punk, metal, EDM and sound horeg, synthesised in-game, plus an announcer.
- **Fighter creator**, costumes, poses, intros, emotes, banners, 42 achievements, levels, crowd reputation.
- **Bilingual**: English and Bahasa Indonesia throughout.

Documentation: **[docs/en](docs/en/README.md)** — getting started, how to play, fighters, arenas,
architecture, asset pipeline, online play, packaging.

## Bahasa Indonesia

Tarkam Arena adalah game pertarungan 3D dari dunia *tarkam* — turnamen antar kampung di Indonesia —
tempat pekerja jalanan, makhluk gaib, satwa, satire, dan legenda Nusantara bertemu.

- **51 petarung**: profesi (gembel lampu merah, kasir minimarket, preman pasar…), makhluk mistis
  (Kuntilanak, Pocong, Buto Ijo, Leak, Kuyang, Ogoh-ogoh, Reog…), satwa Nusantara (orang utan, komodo,
  harimau Sumatra, cenderawasih…), warga & satire (pejabat korup, buzzer politik, mahasiswi demo, kang
  ojol, penari sound horeg…), lima legenda rahasia (Nyi Roro Kidul, Gatotkaca, Si Pitung, Sangkuriang,
  Jaka Tarub), dan tiga paket DLC.
- **Sistem pertarungan sungguhan**: frame data, kombo berantai, launcher dan kombo udara, bantingan dan
  throw tech, counter, tangkisan, menghindar, jurus dengan gerakan stik, mode Amarah dan Adrenalin,
  team combo.
- **Jurus pamungkas**: tiga per petarung dengan meter Jurus — panggil makhluk halus, serbuan pendemo,
  senjata raksasa seperti pacul, manipulasi cuaca, angkat & banting, angin topan, rentetan, gempa, dan
  gelombang, masing-masing dengan cut-in dan VFX sendiri.
- **Finisher**: setiap petarung punya 3 Fatality, 2 Brutality, 2 Persahabatan, 1 Ampunan, dan 1
  Finisher Legenda yang butuh sorakan penonton penuh.
- **Mode**: 1 lawan 1, 2 lawan 2, Tag Battle, Bebas Semua, Survival, Mode Cerita sembilan bab, Piala
  Tarkam, Ranked Online dengan rollback netcode, Perang Klan, Pertandingan Kustom, dan Latihan.
- **18 arena yang hidup** — dari Pasar Becek dan Gedung DPR sampai Borobudur, Gunung Bromo, karnaval
  sound horeg, dan malam pengerupukan — dengan warga yang berdagang, bersorak, merekam, kabur saat
  finisher, dan mengenali petarung, serta event arena yang menghantam petarung.
- **Suara Nusantara**: gamelan, angklung, degung, talempong, sasando, baleganjur, reog, dangdut koplo,
  punk, metal, EDM, dan sound horeg yang disintesis di dalam game, ditambah announcer.
- **Kreator petarung**, kostum, pose, intro, emote, banner, 42 pencapaian, level, reputasi penonton.
- **Dua bahasa**: English dan Bahasa Indonesia di seluruh game.

Dokumentasi: **[docs/id](docs/id/README.md)** — memulai, cara bermain, petarung, arena, arsitektur,
pipeline aset, bermain online, pengemasan.

## Quick start / Mulai cepat

```bash
dotnet build TarkamArena.slnx
dotnet test tests/TarkamArena.Tests
dotnet run --project src/TarkamArena.App
```
